Projects

We study how floods, wildfires, and other climate hazards affect infrastructure and communities. Projects include levee monitoring, coastal erosion, and access to wildfire shelters.

5 current projects

4 completed projects

current · 2026 · Massachusetts Office of Coastal Zone Management

Aquinnah Cliffs erosion and cultural-landscape resilience

The project combines physics-based models and remote sensing to study cliff erosion and assess nature-based options that reflect tribal cultural priorities. Massachusetts CZM funds the Wampanoag Tribe of Gay Head (Aquinnah), with Tufts as technical partner.

Project page

current · 2026 · National Science Foundation

Transforming Levee Monitoring and Risk Assessment by Translating Hybrid Digital Twin Innovation into Practice

The team combines physics-based models and machine learning in a levee digital twin for flood-risk monitoring, working with federal, district, and industry partners. Vahedifard leads NSF TTP-T award 2552816 at Tufts with Babak Moaveni.

Project page

current · 2025 · National Science Foundation

Resilient and Affordable Housing for Rural Communities

This SCC-IRG project develops a platform for housing resilience in rural Mississippi Delta and Gulf Coast counties. Vahedifard is co-PI on NSF award 2530559, awarded to Mississippi State University.

Project page

current · 2024 · NASA

Integrating Earth Observations Toward Advancing Equitable Resilience to Geohazards in a Changing Climate

The team works with the Wampanoag Tribe of Gay Head (Aquinnah) and East Boston’s NOAH to develop tools that combine Earth observations and socioeconomic data to address unequal geohazard risk. A public award number is not yet available.

Project page

current · 2023 · National Science Foundation

Extinguishing the Disparity of Access to Shelters for Disadvantaged Communities in Wildfire-Prone Areas

The project studies unequal access to shelters in wildfire-prone communities. Vahedifard is co-PI on NSF award 2242647, awarded to George Mason University.

Project page

completed · 2023 · National Science Foundation

Equitable Adaptive Strategies for Flood Protection Infrastructure under Current and Future Compound Hazards

This CLIMA collaboration studied equitable levee adaptation to compound climate hazards. Vahedifard led the Tufts component under NSF award 2401545, with related awards 2332262 and 2332263.

Project page

completed · 2020 · National Science Foundation

SCC-PG: Reducing the Vulnerability of Disadvantaged Communities to the Impacts of Cascading Hazards under a Changing Climate

This planning grant examined disadvantaged communities’ vulnerability to cascading climate hazards. The work was conducted during Vahedifard’s time at Mississippi State University.

Project page

completed · 2020 · National Science Foundation

RAPID: Collecting critical data for advancing our understanding of wildfire impacts on soil characteristics and research on post-wildfire compound hazards

The team collected field data on how wildfires change soil properties to support research on post-fire compound hazards.

Project page

completed · 2016 · National Science Foundation

Collaborative Research: Resilience of Geotechnical Infrastructure under a Changing Climate: Quantitative Assessment for Extreme Events

This project assessed how climate extremes affect the resilience of geotechnical infrastructure. The work was conducted during Vahedifard’s time at Mississippi State University.
