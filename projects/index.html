Projects

Current and completed work on climate-loaded infrastructure, geohazards, and environmental justice. Each entry links to a public project or award record when one is available.

5 current projects

4 completed projects

current · 2026 · Massachusetts Office of Coastal Zone Management

Aquinnah Cliffs erosion and cultural-landscape resilience

Physics-based and remote-sensing models of cliff erosion, with nature-based options that respect tribal cultural priorities. Massachusetts CZM grant to the Wampanoag Tribe of Gay Head (Aquinnah); Tufts as technical partner.

Project page

current · 2026 · National Science Foundation

Transforming Levee Monitoring and Risk Assessment by Translating Hybrid Digital Twin Innovation into Practice

Translates a hybrid physics-plus-ML levee digital twin into operational flood-risk monitoring with federal, district, and industry partners. NSF TTP-T 2552816; Tufts PI with Babak Moaveni.

Project page

current · 2025 · National Science Foundation

Resilient and Affordable Housing for Rural Communities

SCC-IRG platform for housing resilience in rural Mississippi Delta and Gulf Coast counties. NSF 2530559; Vahedifard co-PI (MSU awardee).

Project page

current · 2024 · NASA

Integrating Earth Observations Toward Advancing Equitable Resilience to Geohazards in a Changing Climate

Cocreates Earth-observation and socioeconomic tools with the Wampanoag Tribe of Gay Head (Aquinnah) and East Boston’s NOAH to reduce inequitable geohazard risk. NASA-funded; award number not yet on public pages.

Project page

current · 2023 · National Science Foundation

Extinguishing the Disparity of Access to Shelters for Disadvantaged Communities in Wildfire-Prone Areas

Studies unequal access to wildfire shelters for disadvantaged communities. NSF 2242647; Vahedifard co-PI (George Mason awardee).

Project page

completed · 2023 · National Science Foundation

Equitable Adaptive Strategies for Flood Protection Infrastructure under Current and Future Compound Hazards

CLIMA collaborative award on equitable levee adaptation under compound climate hazards. NSF 2401545 (Tufts PI); related awards 2332262 and 2332263.

Project page

completed · 2020 · National Science Foundation

SCC-PG: Reducing the Vulnerability of Disadvantaged Communities to the Impacts of Cascading Hazards under a Changing Climate

Planning grant on cascading-hazard vulnerability of disadvantaged communities (MSU period).

Project page

completed · 2020 · National Science Foundation

RAPID: Collecting critical data for advancing our understanding of wildfire impacts on soil characteristics and research on post-wildfire compound hazards

Rapid field data on wildfire effects on soils and post-fire compound hazards.
