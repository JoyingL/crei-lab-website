Climate Resilient and Equitable Infrastructure Lab

The resilience and adaptation of critical infrastructure to extreme events in a changing climate.

About the lab

Research areas

Drought, flooding, wildfire, and cascading hazards.

Climate Extremes

We study how drought, flooding, and wildfire affect earthen infrastructure and nearby communities. We examine compound and cascading hazards to understand how one event changes the impacts of another.

droughtfloodwildfirecascading hazards

Slopes, dams, and levees in a changing climate.

Earthen Infrastructure

We analyze how changes in moisture and temperature affect the performance of slopes, dams, and levees. This work informs the assessment and adaptation of earthen infrastructure under changing climate conditions.

slopesdamsleveesclimate adaptation

Infrastructure risk and environmental justice.

Equitable Infrastructure

We examine how infrastructure risks affect disadvantaged communities. Our research connects climate adaptation with environmental justice, considering who is exposed to hazards and who benefits from infrastructure protection.

environmental justiceequitycommunities

Water, heat, and stress in unsaturated soils.

Unsaturated Mechanics

Water flow, heat transfer, and stress jointly influence soil behavior and the stability of earthen structures. We study these coupled processes in unsaturated soils using analytical and numerical methods.
