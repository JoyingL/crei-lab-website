About the lab

Research areas

Drought, flood, wildfire, and what follows.

Climate Extremes

Extreme events in a changing climate, including compound and cascading sequences, reshape both communities and the infrastructure they rely on. We study those impacts at the point where climate loading meets earthen systems.

droughtfloodwildfirecascading hazards

Slopes, dams, and levees under nonstationary climate.

Earthen Infrastructure

Critical earthen infrastructure is a first-order climate asset, not a background geotechnical detail. We analyze how slopes, dams, and levees lose performance as wetting, drying, and heat accumulate.

slopesdamsleveesclimate adaptation

Who is behind the levee is part of the problem.

Equitable Infrastructure

Resilience work that does not name disadvantaged communities is incomplete. We frame climate adaptation so that equity is a design constraint, not a separate outreach paragraph.

environmental justiceequitycommunities

Variably saturated soils under multi-physics processes.

Unsaturated Mechanics

The lab’s fundamental geotechnical work is unsaturated soil mechanics and multi-physics processes in soils, closed with analytical and numerical methods. That is the method stack behind the climate and equity questions above.

Highlighted projects

current · 2026 · National Science Foundation

Transforming Levee Monitoring and Risk Assessment by Translating Hybrid Digital Twin Innovation into Practice

Translates a hybrid physics-plus-ML levee digital twin into operational flood-risk monitoring with federal, district, and industry partners. NSF TTP-T 2552816; Tufts PI with Babak Moaveni.

Project page

current · 2024 · NASA

Integrating Earth Observations Toward Advancing Equitable Resilience to Geohazards in a Changing Climate

Cocreates Earth-observation and socioeconomic tools with the Wampanoag Tribe of Gay Head (Aquinnah) and East Boston’s NOAH to reduce inequitable geohazard risk. NASA-funded; award number not yet on public pages.

Project page

completed · 2023 · National Science Foundation

Equitable Adaptive Strategies for Flood Protection Infrastructure under Current and Future Compound Hazards

CLIMA collaborative award on equitable levee adaptation under compound climate hazards. NSF 2401545 (Tufts PI); related awards 2332262 and 2332263.
