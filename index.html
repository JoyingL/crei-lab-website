Climate Resilient and Equitable Infrastructure Lab

The resilience and adaptation of critical infrastructure to extreme events in a changing climate.

About the lab

Research areas

Climate Extremes

How drought, floods, and wildfire interact and affect infrastructure and communities.

Earthen Infrastructure

The performance and adaptation of slopes, dams, and levees under changing climate conditions.

Equitable Infrastructure

Who is exposed to infrastructure risks, and who benefits from protection and adaptation.

Unsaturated Mechanics

How water, heat, and stress interact in unsaturated soils and influence their behavior.
