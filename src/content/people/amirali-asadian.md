Amirali Asadian is a PhD student in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Tufts University. He uses extreme value theory, machine learning, and GIS to study wildfire extremes and soil cracking. He earned his MS at Mississippi State University, after graduate coursework in geotechnical engineering at the University of Tehran and a BS in Civil Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology. A dedicated teaching assistant, he received Tufts’ 2026 Outstanding Graduate Contributor to Engineering Education award. Outside the lab, Amirali plays volleyball, swims, and enjoys traveling/hiking with friends.
