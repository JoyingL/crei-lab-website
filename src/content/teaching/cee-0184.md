---
title: Resilient and Equitable Infrastructure
code: CEE-0184
year: 2026
description: Influence of uncertain extreme weather events on infrastructure. Framework for designing, evaluating and tracking risk-based equitable adaptation strategies to ensure the resiliency of infrastructure under current and future climates. Students will select an infrastructure sector, use open data to examine current technical and equity challenges, and identify climate adaptation strategies that are technically and environmentally sound, socially just, and economically practical.
